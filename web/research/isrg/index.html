01 / UNDER THE HOOD

Three assumptions to challenge.

These are model choices, not company promises.

EARNINGS

Normalized earnings multiple

49.1×

The earnings component has a 66% weight. This premium multiple needs durable growth; it is not a bargain multiple.

GROWTH

Normalized growth input

20.3%

A point-in-time financial-trend input. The DCF forecast fades growth over five years; it does not assume this rate forever.

RISK

Cost of equity

10.0%

The FCFE DCF has a 34% weight and a 2.61% terminal growth assumption. A higher required return lowers its value.

The $442.83 estimate combines normalized earnings and FCFE DCF. The standalone DCF is $187.95 per share. Share price is a comparison, not an input to model fair value.

02 / THE UPDATE

The estimate rose. Here is why.

Between the 22 Apr 2026 and 21 Jul 2026 model nodes, forward revenue and modeled free cash flow increased. The blended estimate rose from $415.95 to $442.83.

These are model-forward inputs—not a claim that management issued equivalent full-year guidance. The figures compare two reporting nodes under the same valuation method.

The $26.88 increase breaks into +$19.15 from the weighted earnings component and +$7.73 from DCF. Growth and the discount rate stayed unchanged; margins, shares and the target multiple also moved.

03 / THE COUNTERCASE

What if the premium is not earned?

Growth disappoints. Investors pay less.

Illustrative stress test: normalized earnings and the entire projected FCFE stream fall 15%, and the earnings multiple compresses from 49.1× to 35×. Keep the same weights, discount rate, terminal growth and share count. This is a scenario—not a probability-weighted forecast.

Valuation formula, inputs and limitations

Base estimate: 66% × Normalized earnings $574.12 + 34% × Five-year FCFE DCF $187.95 = $442.83 per share.

  • Forward revenue $13.27B; normalized net margin 31.11%; quoted-security shares 353.278 million.
  • Earnings value = forward revenue × normalized net margin ÷ shares × 49.1181.
  • Forward model FCFE $3.93B. Discount explicit year-end flows and the terminal value at the cost of equity. No separate dividend or buyback value is added.
  • Model version: pit-valuation-v55-actual-value-and-owner-audit-2026-08-30.
  • The historical green line is a constant-method replay on point-in-time inputs. It must not be interpreted as a published contemporaneous forecast or a backtest of trading returns.
