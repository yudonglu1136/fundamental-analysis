## Primary evidence checked for the competition reply

- [General Mills at AIPCon 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_iIiVtKLX8) and Palantir’s [companion impact study](https://www.palantir.com/assets/xrfr7uokpv1b/1aLBn65y83vdytjpXJKZcO/16989b788b34cb677f6d763d56a72349/Building_an_Intelligent_AI-Driven_Supply_Chain_at_General_Mills_-_AIPCon_March_-24_Impact_Study.pdf): the customer-presented case reports more than 70% of supply-chain recommendations accepted and approximately $40,000 of daily savings. Treat these as customer/issuer-presented results, not independently audited figures.
- [Panasonic Energy of North America at AIPCon 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nUlnb1N6H9E): a customer demonstration of an AIP-backed mixed-reality copilot for field technicians.
- [Lowe’s at AIPCon 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eDH5qeCLiAA) and Palantir’s [official transcript post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/palantir-technologies_using-aip-to-improve-customer-service-lowes-activity-7174864503455559680-pMm2): the customer says the workflow moved from proof of concept to production in under four months, reduced overdue activities by 75%, and reached roughly 1,000 simultaneous users. Treat these as customer statements.
- NHS England’s [Federated Data Platform contract award notice](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/037776-2023): a competitive-dialogue procurement with seven tenders and award weights of 60% quality, 10% social value, and 30% price; Palantir received the £182.2 million award. The notice states that the potential £330 million forecast is not committed.
- The U.S. Army’s [TITAN prototype award](https://cpeisw.army.mil/2024/03/06/army-tactical-intelligence-targeting-access-node-titan-ground-station-prototype-award/): Palantir was selected after a competitive prototype phase against Raytheon that included operational requirements, Soldier Touch Points, and a capstone evaluation. The $178.4 million award covers ten prototypes.
- Palantir’s [2025 Form 10-K](https://investors.palantir.com/files/2025%20FY%20PLTR%2010-K.pdf?mod=article_inline) says its markets are highly competitive and may become more competitive. This is important counter-evidence: the reply argues that deployment depth makes Palantir difficult to displace in selected workflows, not that competition does not exist.

These are representative public examples, not a claim that every Palantir video, contract, or customer outcome has been independently audited. The “mission-critical” conclusion is ThesisForge’s interpretation of the deployment and procurement evidence.
