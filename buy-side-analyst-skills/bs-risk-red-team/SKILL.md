# Investment thesis red-team workflow

## Inputs
- User thesis, memo, model, valuation, position size, time horizon, risk tolerance, and source materials.

## Process
1. Restate the thesis in falsifiable terms.
2. Identify the five strongest ways the thesis can fail: business, financial, accounting, management, competitive, regulatory, macro, valuation, liquidity, and timing.
3. Build a kill memo: what evidence would make a disciplined investor pass or exit.
4. Stress test valuation and assumptions. Identify breakpoints where upside/downside flips.
5. Identify hidden correlations and portfolio-level risks if context is supplied.
6. Rank risks by probability, severity, detectability, and time-to-materialize.
7. Produce a monitoring plan with leading indicators.

## Output format
- Red-team verdict
- Strongest bear case
- Kill criteria
- Assumption breakpoints
- Risk register
- Monitoring triggers
- Questions to resolve before capital is committed

Be adversarial but fair. Do not cherry-pick only negative evidence; include mitigating evidence where supported.
