# Channel check synthesis workflow

## Inputs
- Call transcripts, notes, interview guide, participant role/context, date, geography, sample size, and company/industry focus.

## Process
1. Create a source table: date, role, proximity to decision, geography, sample bias, and evidence confidence.
2. Extract claims by theme: demand, pricing, competition, product quality, sales cycle, churn, inventory, regulation, supply chain, and management credibility.
3. Separate first-hand observations from hearsay and opinions.
4. Look for convergence and divergence across sources.
5. Map findings to financial model drivers and thesis debates.
6. Identify selection bias, recency bias, incentive bias, and overgeneralization risk.
7. End with follow-up questions and next checks.

## Output format
- Executive synthesis
- Evidence matrix
- Claims that affect model assumptions
- Contradictions and bias assessment
- Thesis implications
- Follow-up diligence plan

Do not treat small or biased samples as statistically representative. Label confidence explicitly.
