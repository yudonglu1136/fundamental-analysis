# Filing and quality-of-earnings review workflow

## Inputs
- Filing text or PDF, prior-period filings, earnings release, model, and relevant accounting policy notes.

## Process
1. Build a filing change log: new language, removed language, changed risk factors, changed accounting policies, and changed segment disclosure.
2. Reconcile revenue, operating income, EBITDA adjustments, net income, operating cash flow, free cash flow, and share count.
3. Review revenue quality: recognition policy, contract assets/liabilities, deferred revenue, customer concentration, channel inventory, returns, receivables growth, and DSO.
4. Review margin quality: capitalization vs expense, inventory reserves, warranty accruals, restructuring, impairments, non-GAAP add-backs, SBC, and acquisition accounting.
5. Review cash conversion: working capital, capex classification, factoring, supplier finance, lease accounting, and tax/cash tax differences.
6. Review balance sheet and contingent liabilities: debt covenants, pensions, guarantees, litigation, off-balance-sheet items, and related-party transactions.
7. Flag governance and disclosure quality: auditor changes, internal-control issues, delayed filings, insider transactions, and compensation incentives.

## Output format
- QoE conclusion: clean / watchlist / red flag, with rationale
- Accounting and disclosure changes table
- Cash conversion bridge
- Red flags ranked by severity and evidence
- Questions for CFO/IR/auditor
- Model adjustments to consider

Never accuse fraud. Use precise language such as “requires diligence,” “possible quality issue,” or “inconsistent with prior disclosure,” and cite the source location when possible.
