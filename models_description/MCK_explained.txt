1. What This Module Is

The MCK module is a platform-native McKesson research system inside the
fundamental-analysis app. It replaces the old workbook snapshot model with a
business-specific buy-side workbench for a low-margin, high-turnover,
cash-generative healthcare distributor with meaningful specialty / oncology,
biopharma services, prescription technology, and buyback-driven per-share
compounding.

It is loaded through:

- src/stocks/registry.ts
- src/stocks/mck/config.ts
- src/stocks/mck/dashboard.tsx

The module contract remains:

- data
- calculateSummary
- calculateValuation
- Dashboard
- valuationConfig

2. Why MCK Is Not Modeled Like LSEG

LSEG is modeled as a financial data/workflow and infrastructure company with
subscription, data, index, market infrastructure, and optional strategic SOTP
drivers.

MCK is different. The MCK module focuses on:

- pharmaceutical distribution scale
- margin basis points rather than gross-margin optics
- specialty and oncology profit growth
- manufacturer / biopharma service infrastructure
- prescription technology and access / affordability platforms
- working-capital volatility
- FCF conversion over a full cycle
- share repurchase accretion
- SOTP multiple differences by segment

Low gross margin is not treated as a flaw by itself. For MCK, the key question
is whether scale, turns, working-capital management, service revenue, and
buybacks create high per-share compounding despite very thin distribution
margin rates.

4. Engine Graph

The MCK engine graph is intentionally business-specific:

- segmentEconomicsEngine
  Breaks revenue, adjusted operating profit, margin bps, profit mix, moat score,
  risk level, and SOTP multiple by segment.

- distributionEconomicsEngine
  Treats North American Pharmaceutical as a low-margin scale engine. It shows
  revenue base, margin bps, operating leverage, GLP-1 caveats, working-capital
  intensity, and EPS/FCF sensitivity for every margin-bps change.

- specialtyOncologyEngine
  Builds the core investment-thesis area around Oncology & Multispecialty,
  community oncology, practice-management stickiness, specialty distribution,
  biosimilars, and manufacturer service loops.

- prescriptionTechnologyEngine
  Separates RxTS from distribution and tracks whether access, affordability,
  workflow, 3PL, and manufacturer connectivity deserve a higher multiple.

- biopharmaServicesEngine
  Frames manufacturer-facing services, hub services, patient support, data, and
  launch support as healthcare service infrastructure rather than simple
  distribution volume.

- workingCapitalEngine
  Separates reported FCF from normalized FCF and warns against mechanical
  annualization of one quarter.

- marginBridgeEngine
  Shows basis-point drivers: branded mix, generic economics, specialty /
  oncology, GLP-1, cost inflation, compliance, and operating leverage.

- buybackEngine
  Converts FCF capacity, dividend payout, buyback amount, average repurchase
  price, and share count into share reduction, EPS accretion, FCF/share
  accretion, and 3-year / 5-year cumulative repurchase impact.

- capitalAllocationEngine
  Shows FCF use across dividends, buybacks, M&A capacity, net debt, and
  authorization.

- valuationEngine
  Triangulates P/E, FCF yield, owner-earnings DCF, and operating SOTP.

- scenarioEngine
  Produces bear/base/bull 3-year and 5-year TSR outputs from EPS CAGR, exit P/E,
  dividend yield, buyback assumptions, margin changes, and downside shocks.

- riskEngine
  Scores reimbursement / PBM pressure, customer concentration, opioid
  litigation, working-capital volatility, generic deflation, government pricing,
  DSCSA / compliance, and capital allocation mistakes.

- earningsCallEngine
  Keeps the past eight quarters of earnings-call / earnings-release events,
  market focus, management tone, topic trends, management quotes, and Q&A
  pressure points research-only. The dashboard provides a horizontal scroll bar
  to select a quarter and an AI synthesis of how market concerns changed over
  the last eight quarters.

5. Valuation Methods

A. EPS / P/E

Uses forward adjusted EPS and a target P/E multiple. This is a cross-check, not
the entire valuation.

B. FCF Yield

Capitalizes normalized FCF per share at a target FCF yield. This is important
because MCK is a FCF and buyback compounder.

C. Owner-Earnings DCF

Uses normalized owner earnings, explicit growth, WACC, terminal growth, net debt
deduction, and a working-capital normalization adjustment. The DCF is designed
to show why a low-margin distributor can still compound per-share value when
cash generation and buybacks are durable.

D. SOTP

Applies different multiples to different profit pools:

- North American Pharmaceutical: lower distribution multiple
- Oncology & Multispecialty: higher specialty / ecosystem multiple
- Prescription Technology Solutions: higher platform-like multiple
- Medical-Surgical Solutions: separate lower multiple
- corporate / other and net debt adjustments

The model does not apply one consolidated P/E multiple to all businesses.

E. TSR Scenario Lab

Calculates 3-year and 5-year target price / IRR under bear, base, and bull
cases. It includes EPS CAGR, ending multiple, dividend yield, buyback impact,
margin bps pressure/expansion, and regulatory/legal downside shock.

6. Research-Only Boundaries

The following do not directly feed valuation unless explicitly promoted by an
analyst:

- management quotes
- Q&A pressure points
- transcript themes
- moat score
- risk score
- market narrative
- analyst concern tags
- GLP-1 qualitative assessment
- biosimilar qualitative tracker

These fields are displayed to improve research judgment, not to silently alter
valuation.

7. What Not To Do

Do not read MCK like a generic healthcare company.

Do not conclude that low gross margin means low quality. MCK is built on scale,
turns, channel reliability, compliance, service layers, FCF, and share-count
shrink.

Do not over-focus on revenue growth. Revenue is enormous, but the model focuses
on adjusted operating profit, margin bps, working capital, FCF conversion,
share repurchase, and per-share value.

Do not annualize one quarter of FCF. Working capital can distort reported cash
flow.

Do not treat GLP-1 as automatically good or bad. The model separates volume,
margin rate, inventory, mix, reimbursement, and working-capital effects.

Do not let transcript-derived evidence directly change valuation assumptions.

Do not treat buybacks as always value-creating. Repurchase ROIC falls when the
average buyback price rises above underwriting value.

8. Validation

Run:

- npm run validate:mck

The validation checks:

- segment revenue reconciliation
- segment operating profit sanity
- margin bps calculations
- EPS bridge positivity
- FCF conversion range
- share-count reduction from buybacks
- SOTP duplicate segment risk
- DCF terminal value dominance
- WACC / terminal growth spread
- placeholder visibility for share count and net debt

The TypeScript app should also pass:

- npm run typecheck
- npm run build

9. Maintenance

To refresh official data:

- npm run mck:fetch-official
- npm run mck:build-metrics

To ingest transcripts:

- place transcript .txt, .md, or .html files in data/local/mck/transcripts/raw/
- npm run mck:fetch-transcripts
- npm run mck:build-qa-pairs

The current earnings-call screen includes eight release-derived quarters:

- FY2026 Q4
- FY2026 Q3
- FY2026 Q2
- FY2026 Q1
- FY2025 Q4
- FY2025 Q3
- FY2025 Q2
- FY2025 Q1

For those quarters, quarterly financial highlights and market-focus summaries
come from official earnings releases. Full Q&A remains pending unless local
transcript files are ingested.

To extend the model:

- add new official data in src/stocks/mck/data/official/
- add assumptions in src/stocks/mck/assumptions.ts
- add calculation logic in src/stocks/mck/engines/
- expose research output in src/stocks/mck/components/
- update src/stocks/mck/validation/ if the new logic changes model mechanics

Keep source type, confidence, and placeholder status explicit. That discipline
is what keeps the module usable for real buy-side work instead of becoming a
pretty dashboard with mixed-quality inputs.
