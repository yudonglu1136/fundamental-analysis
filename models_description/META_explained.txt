1. META Module Design

The META module is a platform-native stock module registered through
src/stocks/registry.ts. It preserves the common contract:

- data
- calculateSummary
- calculateValuation
- Dashboard
- valuationConfig

The module is no longer an additive AI uplift dashboard. It is a buy-side
research cockpit for internet advertising economics, AI monetization,
infrastructure capex-to-ROIC, product engagement, regulatory risk, and Reality
Labs option value.

2. Why META Needs A Different Cockpit

META should not be analyzed like a generic software company or as a simple P/E
page. The core debates are:

- whether Family of Apps can keep growing through impressions and price per ad;
- whether AI tools improve conversion, advertiser ROAS, and auction quality;
- whether the AI infrastructure buildout earns enough return on capital;
- whether Reality Labs losses are tolerable relative to the option value;
- whether regulation and privacy changes impair targeting data;
- whether buybacks offset SBC and help EPS without masking FCF pressure.

5. Engine Map

- forecastEngine: builds five-year revenue, FoA operating income, Reality Labs
  loss, capex, D&A, working capital, unlevered FCF, EPS, share count, AI payback,
  and AI ROIC.
- adEconomicsEngine: reconciles advertising revenue to impressions x price per
  ad and maps AI ad commentary to model drivers.
- aiCapexEngine: tracks capex guidance, capex step-up, infrastructure
  commitments, AI ROIC, and payback.
- valuationEngine: calculates DCF, FCF yield, P/E, EV/EBIT, SOTP, scenario fair
  values, and sensitivities.
- riskRedTeamEngine: ranks AI capex, regulation, privacy, competition, Reality
  Labs, and model-commoditization risks.
- validationEngine: checks segment reconciliation, FCF bridge, ad bridge,
  capex guidance, valuation weights, terminal value, AI uplift isolation, and
  SBC/buyback share-count treatment.

6. Valuation Framework

The base fair value is a weighted blend:

- DCF: unlevered FCFF, total capex charged, net cash added after EV.
- FCF yield: normalized FCF/share capitalized at a target yield.
- P/E: normalized EPS after Reality Labs losses and share-count effects.
- EV/EBIT: consolidated operating income cross-check.
- SOTP: Family of Apps EBIT value, less funded Reality Labs drag, plus explicit
  Reality Labs option value and net cash.

AI uplift is not added as a separate premium to base fair value. It is already
embedded through revenue growth, price per ad, FoA margin, and capex fade. AI
excess return is shown as a diagnostic only.

Reality Labs option value appears only in SOTP. Consolidated DCF, P/E, and
EV/EBIT already include the loss drag.

7. Validation Discipline

scripts/meta_model_validation.mjs checks:

- module registration and stock-module contract;
- FoA + Reality Labs revenue reconciliation to consolidated revenue;
- FoA + Reality Labs operating income reconciliation to consolidated operating
  income;
- FCF = operating cash flow less capex including finance leases;
- advertising revenue growth bridge from impressions x average price per ad;
- FY2026 capex assumption versus management guidance;
- valuation weights sum to 100%;
- terminal value concentration;
- AI uplift is diagnostic and not double counted;
- SBC/buyback affect share count rather than a standalone value add;
- required source-status categories and research-only isolation;
- finite valuation output.
- eight-quarter earnings-call coverage and AI-assisted market-focus trend
  synthesis.

7A. Earnings Call Cockpit

The Earnings Calls tab adds the latest eight quarterly call records from Q2
2024 through Q1 2026. The quarter selector is intentionally horizontal and
scrollable so the user can scan the time series and click into any quarter.

The overview does not invent consensus numbers. It summarizes official
transcript context and curated buy-side call intelligence across:

- ad momentum;
- AI monetization;
- AI capex concern;
- engagement and product cycle;
- regulation and privacy;
- Reality Labs tolerance;
- capital return, SBC, and FCF per share.

The AI synthesis is research-only. It is used to explain how market focus
changed over time; it does not add a separate valuation premium. Any selected
quarter's discussion must flow into valuation through named assumptions such as
pricePerAdCagr, adImpressionCagr, aiRevenueUpliftPct, capex2026,
terminalCapexIntensity, regulatoryRevenueHaircut, or realityLabsAnnualLoss.

8. Current Limitations

- Meta does not disclose AI-only capex, so AI capex share is a forecast
  assumption.
- WhatsApp revenue is not separately disclosed, so WhatsApp remains a product
  signal rather than a standalone base valuation segment.
- Market price is a dated snapshot and should be refreshed before relying on
  live upside/downside.
- Peer multiples are not yet populated.
- Transcript insights are curated rather than fully parsed from PDF tables.

9. Institutional Upgrade Plan - May 2026

Audit findings before the second upgrade:

- The first version separates official actuals, management guidance, forecast
  assumptions, research-only material, and market data, but most critical
  numeric fields do not yet carry field-level lineage. The model needs a
  reusable DataLineage object with source type, source name, URL, filing type,
  period, as-of date, retrieval date, confidence, and valuation treatment.
- Scenario assumptions are model-critical but under-governed. Revenue CAGR,
  impression CAGR, price-per-ad CAGR, FoA margin, capex, terminal capex
  intensity, WACC, buyback, SBC, and Reality Labs option value must have
  metadata showing whether they are official anchors, forecast assumptions, or
  research-only judgement calls.
- Product-cycle data is currently a signal table. It needs explicit mapping to
  valuation drivers and a validation rule that prevents product signals from
  being capitalized unless they pass through a named driver.
- Risk red-team rows currently rank risk severity. The upgraded model must
  connect risks to valuation haircuts and thesis breakpoints so the dashboard
  can answer which wrong variable invalidates the investment case.
- The ad-economics engine explains ads through impressions and price per ad,
  but it needs a richer attribution bridge: base ad revenue, engagement /
  inventory, pricing / auction quality, AI monetization, regulatory haircut,
  and residual mix/FX.
- The forecast engine needs to anchor 2026 revenue to Q1 actuals and Q2
  management guidance, then show the implied H2 path. It should remove hidden
  hardcoded growth rates where possible and expose them as audited assumptions.
- The valuation engine has complete methods, but it lacks current-price implied
  expectations, driver attribution, and a formal double-count taxonomy. The
  upgrade adds reverse DCF / implied multiple / implied revenue and margin
  diagnostics.
- Validation needs to go beyond arithmetic checks. It should test lineage
  coverage, assumption metadata coverage, product-signal isolation, market data
  freshness, scenario spread, market-implied output, thesis breakpoints, and
  terminal value concentration.

Upgrade execution sequence:

1. Add DataLineage and assumption-metadata types to src/stocks/meta/model.ts.
2. Add src/stocks/meta/data/lineage.ts and attach row-level plus critical
   field-level lineage to official actuals, guidance, segment data, ad
   economics, AI capex, product signals, Reality Labs, risks, transcripts,
   market data, and research-only notes.
3. Add assumption metadata in src/stocks/meta/assumptions.ts so every valuation
   assumption has confidence, as-of date, and valuation treatment.
4. Upgrade forecastEngine to produce a Q1 actual + Q2 guide + H2 implied
   revenue bridge, ad driver attribution, explicit regulatory haircut, SBC
   dollars, buyback dollars, and Reality Labs revenue assumptions.
5. Add marketImpliedEngine, thesisBreakEngine, valuationAttributionEngine, and
   valuationIntegrityEngine.
6. Upgrade adEconomicsEngine, aiCapexEngine, and riskRedTeamEngine so they feed
   valuation diagnostics rather than remaining display-only.
7. Upgrade dashboard tabs to show data lineage, market-implied expectations,
   thesis breakpoints, valuation attribution, and integrity checks.
8. Upgrade scripts/meta_model_validation.mjs and local metric database output to
   validate the new institutional checks.
