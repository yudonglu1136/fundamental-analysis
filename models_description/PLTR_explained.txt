PLTR fundamental research module
================================

Purpose
-------
The PLTR module is a Palantir-specific buy-side research interface. It uses the
existing stock-module contract, but it does not copy LSEG's financial market
infrastructure framework. PLTR is treated as an AI software, ontology,
government mission-critical, and commercial AIP adoption platform.

Research questions
------------------
1. Is PLTR becoming the enterprise AI operating system layer?
2. Is AIP converting hype into measurable commercial revenue?
3. Is US Commercial growth broad-based or concentrated?
4. Is Government still a durable moat or becoming a slower legacy segment?
5. Are margins expanding because of true operating leverage or adjusted metric treatment?
6. Is SBC dilution still a major shareholder risk?
7. What growth and margin assumptions are already priced into the stock?
8. What would make PLTR a great long-term compounder?
9. What would make PLTR a narrative bubble?
10. At what price does the risk/reward become attractive?

Data layers
-----------
actuals:
  Reported quarterly metrics such as revenue, segment revenue, customer count,
  margins, FCF, Rule of 40, SBC, share count, cash, debt, and net cash.

guidance:
  Management guidance for revenue, adjusted operating income, adjusted FCF, and
  US commercial revenue where disclosed.

forecast assumptions:
  Revenue CAGR, segment growth, operating margins, FCF margin, SBC
  normalization, dilution, tax rate, WACC, terminal multiple, net cash, and
  share count.

research-only signals:
  AIP adoption score, ontology moat score, transcript topics, management
  confidence, and risk score. These signals cannot directly change valuation.

valuation outputs:
  Revenue multiple, EV/FCF, DCF, Rule-of-40 implied multiple, long-term FCF per
  share, scenario fair values, expected returns, and reverse DCF requirements.

Business-specific engines
-------------------------
AIP adoption engine:
  Scores US commercial revenue growth, US commercial customer growth,
  commercial revenue per customer, management/research evidence, guidance
  upgrades, production deployment evidence, and cross-sell signals. This score
  is research-only.

Ontology moat engine:
  Scores data integration depth, workflow embedding, permission/governance
  complexity, mission-critical use case, human-in-the-loop decision system,
  cross-department deployment, switching cost, expansion potential, and
  model-agnostic AI orchestration.

Customer cohort engine:
  Tracks customer count, commercial customer count, US commercial customer
  count, revenue per customer, commercial revenue per commercial customer, net
  dollar retention, large deals, and concentration risk gaps.

Rule of 40 / operating leverage engine:
  Tracks revenue growth, adjusted operating margin, GAAP operating margin, FCF
  margin, Rule of 40, and adjusted versus GAAP profitability.

SBC dilution tracker:
  Tracks SBC expense, SBC as percent of revenue, diluted share count, per-share
  FCF, and whether company-level FCF is offset by dilution.

Valuation framework
-------------------
The module uses multiple valuation approaches:
1. Revenue multiple framework.
2. EV / FCF framework.
3. DCF framework.
4. Rule-of-40 implied multiple framework.
5. Scenario-based long-term FCF per share framework.

The reverse DCF asks what five-year revenue CAGR, FCF margin, and terminal FCF
multiple are required to justify the selected market price.

Validation
----------
Validation covers:
1. Data completeness, source URLs, duplicated periods, negative values, and unit
   mismatches.
2. Segment revenue reconciliation, growth/margin/FCF/SBC consistency, and
   per-share metric share-count use.
3. Valuation consistency for net cash, share count, WACC, terminal growth, and
   reverse DCF outputs.
4. Research-only separation. AIP score, ontology score, transcript sentiment,
   and Q&A data must not enter valuation directly.

Known limitations in this first version
---------------------------------------
The starter dataset is not institution-grade. Several values are seeded from
official presentation charts or derived from rounded values. Run:

  npm run pltr:fetch-official
  npm run pltr:build-metrics
  npm run pltr:fetch-transcripts
  npm run pltr:build-qa-pairs
  npm run pltr:validate

Then manually review the generated metric database before promoting any local
data into curated actuals.
