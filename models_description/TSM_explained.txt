TSM module explained
====================

Purpose
-------
The TSM module is a TSMC-specific buy-side research cockpit. It is not a generic semiconductor model and should not be copied from NVDA. TSMC is the scaled pure-play foundry layer behind advanced logic manufacturing, not a GPU designer.

Core investor questions
-----------------------
1. Is AI/HPC demand structurally durable enough to justify elevated capex?
2. Can TSMC sustain extraordinary gross and operating margins as N2 and overseas fabs ramp?
3. Is advanced-node and advanced-packaging scarcity creating pricing power, or only a temporary cycle?
4. How much of growth is broad foundry demand versus AI/HPC pull-forward?
5. What explicit geopolitical, customer concentration and AI-cycle haircuts are needed?
6. At what ADR price does FCF per ADR provide a margin of safety?

Data layering
-------------
official_actual:
- TSMC quarterly results pages.
- TSMC 2025 annual report.

management_guidance:
- Q2 2026 revenue, gross margin and operating margin midpoint seeded from the Q1 2026 quarterly results page.

market_data_proxy:
- Current ADR price and ADR-equivalent shares are placeholders until a backend daily_price_bars table is built.

research_only:
- Residual platform mix and capex-cycle interpretation.

Valuation framework
-------------------
The initial valuation triangulates:
- DCF / FCFF.
- FCF yield.
- P/E.
- EV / EBIT.
- Node-mix SOTP.

Research-only technology and platform scores do not directly change valuation. They must be mapped explicitly into revenue growth, margin, capex intensity, or risk haircuts.

Known limitations
-----------------
- No TSM backend SQLite module yet.
- No daily price bars, historical valuation runs, or backtest yet.
- No full multi-year quarterly official financial database yet.
- Capex guidance is included as a research-cycle variable and should be backfilled from official quarterly management reports.
