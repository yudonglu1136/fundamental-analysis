1. BA.L Module Design

The BA.L module is a platform-native stock module for BAE Systems plc. It is
registered through src/stocks/registry.ts and follows the same stock-module
contract as LSEG:

- data
- calculateSummary
- calculateValuation
- Dashboard
- valuationConfig

The module lives under src/stocks/ba because existing modules use lowercase
module directories while the registry exposes the display ticker. The registry
key is "BA.L".

2. Why BAE Needs A Defense Cockpit

BAE should not be analyzed like a generic industrial. The core questions are:

- how much future revenue is supported by order backlog;
- whether order intake remains above sales;
- which segments convert backlog into margin and cash;
- whether defence-budget commitments survive political and fiscal cycles;
- whether long-cycle programmes create durable economics or execution risk;
- whether FCF/share can compound after capex, working capital, pensions,
  leases, and shareholder returns.

The dashboard is therefore organized as a "BAE Systems Defense Research
Cockpit" with tabs for executive summary, segment intelligence, backlog,
defence-cycle scenarios, programme matrix, valuation triangulation, risk red
team, and dividend/buyback quality.

3. Engine Map

- segmentEngine: reconciles segment sales, EBIT, order intake, and backlog to
  group data; scores segment quality using margin, growth, backlog coverage,
  and cash conversion.
- backlogEngine: calculates total backlog, backlog growth, order intake,
  book-to-bill, backlog coverage, segment backlog, major contract wins, and
  revenue visibility.
- defenseCycleEngine: maps macro/geopolitical drivers into bull/base/bear
  scenario assumptions. News or policy narratives are not direct valuation
  inputs.
- programExposureEngine: classifies Typhoon, GCAP/Tempest, submarines, Type 26,
  SSN-AUKUS, missile defence, combat vehicles, munitions, and cyber exposure by
  maturity, margin attractiveness, growth contribution, and risk.
- valuationEngine: builds FCFF DCF, FCF yield, P/E, EV/EBIT, and backlog
  durability-adjusted fair value.
- moatEngine: scores government relationships, long-cycle programmes,
  classified capability, switching cost, sovereign industrial base, technical
  complexity, installed base, backlog visibility, and mission criticality.
- riskRedTeamEngine: ranks risks by probability, impact, detectability, affected
  valuation driver, and mitigation.
- dividendEngine: analyzes dividend per share, growth, payout, FCF payout,
  buybacks, total shareholder returns, and sustainability.
- reportingEventEngine: organizes the last eight quarter windows of official
  BAE reporting events, including results videos where available and official
  market updates where no quarterly call transcript exists. It produces a
  research-only AI synthesis of how market focus has changed over time.

5. Official Actual Data

Official actuals include FY2025 and FY2024 sales, revenue, underlying EBIT,
operating profit, EPS, FCF, operating cash flow, order intake, order backlog,
order book, dividend per share, net debt excluding leases, lease liabilities,
post-employment benefit surplus, share count, shareholder returns, and segment
financial data where disclosed.

6. Management Guidance

FY2026 guidance is stored separately from actuals:

- sales growth range: 7% to 9%
- underlying EBIT growth range: 9% to 11%
- underlying EPS growth range: 9% to 11%
- FCF floor: greater than GBP1.3bn
- effective tax rate: c.22%
- underlying net finance costs: c.GBP370m
- NCI: c.GBP80m
- segment sales and return-on-sales guidance
- GBP/USD sensitivity

7. Forecast Assumptions

Forecast assumptions live in src/stocks/ba/assumptions.ts and valuationConfig.
They include revenue CAGR, operating margin, tax rate, D&A intensity, capex
intensity, working-capital drag, WACC, terminal growth, target FCF yield, target
P/E, target EV/EBIT, share count, balance-sheet bridge, dividend per share, and
valuation weights.

8. Research-Only Notes

Programme, moat, defence-cycle driver, and risk notes are marked research-only.
They support interpretation, filters, scorecards, and scenario mapping. They do
not overwrite official actuals and do not directly feed valuation.

9. Valuation Framework

The blend is deliberately not a simple average:

- DCF: 35%
- FCF yield: 25%
- EV/EBIT: 10%
- P/E: 10%
- Backlog durability-adjusted core value: 20%

DCF uses unlevered FCF and deducts net debt excluding leases plus lease
liabilities after enterprise value, then credits the IAS 19 post-employment
benefit surplus. FCF yield uses normalized equity FCF rather than a single
customer-advance-heavy year. EV/EBIT and P/E are cross-checks.

The backlog layer does not directly capitalize programme headlines. It adjusts a
capped core value using backlog coverage, book-to-bill, and backlog durability.

10. Scenarios

Bear case:

- lower revenue CAGR;
- lower margin;
- higher WACC;
- lower P/E and EV/EBIT;
- higher target FCF yield;
- procurement delay and budget fatigue.

Base case:

- starts from 2026 management guidance;
- normalizes toward high-single-digit then mid-single-digit growth;
- stable margin;
- normalized cash conversion.

Bull case:

- NATO rearmament persists;
- AUKUS, submarines, missile defence, electronic warfare, and munitions demand
  extend duration;
- backlog conversion is smoother;
- risk discount declines.

10A. Reporting Event Intelligence

BAE follows a UK disclosure cadence rather than a US-style quarterly earnings
call cadence. The cockpit therefore uses eight quarter windows anchored to the
official events that occurred in those windows:

- 2024 Q3: 2024 half-year results
- 2024 Q4: November 2024 market update
- 2025 Q1: 2024 full-year results
- 2025 Q2: May 2025 AGM market update
- 2025 Q3: 2025 half-year results
- 2025 Q4: November 2025 market update
- 2026 Q1: 2025 full-year results
- 2026 Q2: May 2026 trading update

The dashboard includes a horizontal scrollbar selector for these quarter
windows. Each event has official-source provenance, transcript/video status,
key metrics where disclosed, management message, debate questions, watch items,
and an AI summary. The AI summary is tagged research-only and is used only to
explain market-focus trends. It does not enter valuation.

The eight-quarter synthesis currently shows a focus shift from backlog and SMS
integration, to cash conversion and customer-advance timing, then to funded
orders, Typhoon / Type 26 / missile defence, defence-budget durability, and
capital-return discipline after the share-price rerating.

11. Validation Logic

scripts/ba_model_validation.mjs checks:

- module registration and stock-module contract;
- segment sales reconciliation to group sales;
- segment EBIT reconciliation to group underlying EBIT;
- backlog coverage = backlog / sales;
- book-to-bill = order intake / sales;
- FCF bridge against operating cash flow minus capex, with a warning because
  BAE's official APM FCF has definition-specific items;
- dividend payout ratios;
- DCF terminal value concentration;
- WACC, terminal growth, margin, and revenue CAGR ranges;
- research-only isolation;
- valuation weights summing to 100%;
- required source-status categories;
- finite fair value output.
- eight reporting-event windows, chronological ordering, source-map coverage,
  explicit transcript/video status, and research-only AI synthesis.

12. Current Limitations

- Direct automated PDF download from BAE may be blocked by the website firewall.
  The fetch script records this instead of pretending extraction succeeded.
- Programme-level revenue and margin are not officially disclosed, so programme
  exposure remains research-only.
- Prior-year segment backlog is not available for every segment in the curated
  dataset.
- Market data is a dated IR page snapshot, not a live institutional feed.
- Peer historical multiple data is not yet populated.
- FCF normalization needs a fuller company-defined APM bridge.
