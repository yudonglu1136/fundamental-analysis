AZN explained
=============

This module is a pharma-specific AstraZeneca research cockpit. It is not a generic financial dashboard. The model asks whether AstraZeneca's protected oncology and rare-disease franchises, CVRM follow-ons, China / Emerging Markets exposure and pipeline rNPV are enough to bridge looming patent cliffs in Farxiga, Lynparza, legacy respiratory products, Brilinta and Soliris.

Why AZN cannot be analyzed only with P/E
----------------------------------------

A single P/E ratio mixes too many different economic layers:

1. current reported IFRS earnings, which include acquisition amortisation and other adjustments;
2. core / adjusted earnings, which management uses for operating comparability;
3. blockbuster drug durability, where patent and reimbursement risk can change cash-flow duration;
4. oncology and rare-disease quality, which deserve different risk and duration assumptions from mature respiratory or CVRM assets;
5. pipeline optionality, which can be very valuable but is not reported revenue; and
6. FX / listing mechanics, because AZN reports in USD while London shares trade in GBX and the US line now represents ordinary shares.

Engine overview
---------------

therapyAreaEngine.ts reconciles reported therapy-area revenue to total revenue and builds the segment dashboard for Oncology, CVRM, Respiratory & Immunology, Infectious Disease, Rare Disease and Other Medicines.

drugDurabilityEngine.ts scores blockbuster durability. The score is explainable: market leadership + patent protection + indication expansion + competitive moat + pricing power + geographic diversification. It is intended to rank revenue quality, not to replace source evidence.

patentCliffEngine.ts converts LOE assumptions into a 2025-2035 timeline, revenue-at-risk bars, regional toggles and cliff-adjusted revenue scenarios.

pipelineEngine.ts values pipeline assets with probability-adjusted scenario math. Official sources support phase and catalyst facts; peak sales and probability of success are research-only assumptions.

earningsCallEngine.ts tracks the past eight quarterly results events from Q2 2024 through Q1 2026. It uses official webcast / PDF availability and builds a display-only AI synthesis of how market focus moved across revenue momentum, guidance, oncology, pipeline, patent / LOE, China, margins, capital allocation and business development. This layer is not allowed to feed valuation directly.

oncologyEngine.ts treats Oncology as AZN's core engine, including revenue bridge, lung/breast/hematology franchises, collaboration economics, pipeline optionality and risk heatmap.

rareDiseaseEngine.ts analyzes the Alexion / Rare Disease franchise, especially the Soliris-to-Ultomiris transition, complement biology durability, orphan pricing and reimbursement risk.

cvrmEngine.ts focuses on Farxiga / dapagliflozin durability, diabetes / HF / CKD indications, GLP-1 adjacency and LOE-adjusted scenarios.

chinaExposureEngine.ts separates China and Emerging Markets from the global total and flags VBP, NRDL, hospital-channel, local competition and regulatory risk.

financialQualityEngine.ts separates reported IFRS metrics from core metrics, then analyzes margin, R&D intensity, FCF conversion, net debt / EBITDA, dividend coverage, ROIC-WACC spread and adjustment quality.

valuationEngine.ts triangulates DCF, therapy-area SOTP, pipeline rNPV and peer multiples. It converts USD model outputs to GBP per London ordinary share and also shows the current NYSE ordinary-share value and historical ADR equivalent for audit only.

riskEngine.ts aggregates patent, clinical, pricing, China, FX, M&A / licensing and competition risk into a monitoring radar.

evidenceEngine.ts builds the audit trail across official, filing, market-data, third-party and research-only sources.

Data layers
-----------

reportedData contains official AstraZeneca results: revenue, therapy-area revenue, geography revenue, key drug revenue, reported EPS, core EPS, cash flow, capex and net debt.

guidanceData contains management guidance, such as Total Revenue growth, Core EPS growth and core tax-rate ranges.

earningsCallData contains the eight-quarter results-event timeline. It stores event date, official source, webcast availability, revenue / core EPS headline metrics, management messages, market-focus themes, AI summaries, topic scores and next-call watchlists. It is explicitly display-only.

marketData contains public market snapshots: AZN.L price in GBX, normalized GBP price, NYSE ordinary-share price, GBP/USD, dividend yield, shares and enterprise value.

pipelineData contains official phase / catalyst facts plus research-only peak-sales and probability-of-success assumptions.

patentRiskData contains LOE / patent-cliff assumptions by product and region, anchored to AstraZeneca patent supplements where available.

researchEstimates contains scenario-only assumptions such as WACC, pipeline rNPV margins, probabilities, risk haircuts and peer multiple adjustments.

evidenceData is the source audit trail. Each item has sourceName, sourceUrl, sourceQuality, period, page, excerpt, confidence, valuationUseAllowed and researchOnly.

What can enter valuation
------------------------

Official reported revenue, product revenue, therapy-area revenue, geography revenue, guidance, cash flow, net debt, dividend and market data can enter valuation directly when the source evidence is marked valuationUseAllowed.

Pipeline peak sales, probability of success, risk haircuts, WACC, terminal growth, therapy-area multiples and peer-multiple adjustments are scenario assumptions. They can enter valuation only as research-only estimates, never as reported facts.

Pipeline rNPV formula
---------------------

probabilityAdjustedPipelineValue =
  peakSales
  x targetPipelineMargin
  x probabilityOfSuccess
  x durationFactor
  x discountFactor

The output is a research estimate. It is probability-adjusted, discounted and kept separate from reported financials.

Patent cliff impact
-------------------

Patent cliffs reduce expected cash-flow duration and can pressure revenue, margin and valuation multiple. The LOE engine estimates revenue at risk by product and region, then shows a cliff-adjusted revenue scenario. It is especially important for Farxiga, Lynparza, Brilinta, Soliris, Symbicort and eventually Tagrisso. Mitigation comes from lifecycle expansion, next-generation assets, oncology growth, rare-disease durability and pipeline replacement value.

Valuation methods
-----------------

DCF is useful for long-term cash-flow compounding and margin expansion. It is sensitive to WACC, terminal growth and FCF conversion.

SOTP is useful because Oncology, CVRM, Respiratory & Immunology, Rare Disease, Infectious Disease and Other Medicines have different growth, margin and patent-risk profiles. It can overstate precision if multiples are treated mechanically.

Pipeline rNPV captures asset optionality. It is necessary for pharma but must be clearly tagged as research-only because official filings do not report peak-sales probabilities.

Peer multiples provide a market cross-check against Roche, Novartis, Sanofi, GSK, Pfizer, Merck, Eli Lilly, Novo Nordisk, AbbVie and Bristol Myers Squibb. They should not be applied mechanically because growth, obesity exposure, oncology mix, patent cliffs and balance sheets differ.

Current limitations
-------------------

The module uses manually curated official extracts and public market snapshots. Some pipeline peak-sales estimates, probabilities, WACC and peer multiple adjustments are research-only and should be debated. Product-level margins are not officially disclosed, so therapy-area margin proxies are scenario estimates. Patent / LOE assumptions rely on public patent supplements and may change with litigation, settlements, regulatory exclusivity or regional label changes. Consensus estimates are not mechanically imported unless a free and reliable public source is available.

How to upgrade toward institution-grade
---------------------------------------

1. Add automated PDF table extraction and source-page citations for each official product and therapy-area table.
2. Add ClinicalTrials.gov / FDA / EMA feeds for trial status, enrollment and approval milestones.
3. Add a full product-level forecast model with country mix, LOE curves, price / volume splits and gross-to-net assumptions.
4. Add peer consensus from a licensed data source and keep it separate from reported facts.
5. Add rNPV curves by asset with royalties, milestones, launch costs, COGS, tax and indication-level probabilities.
6. Add litigation and exclusivity monitoring for Farxiga, Tagrisso, Lynparza, Soliris / Ultomiris and respiratory products.
7. Add China tender / NRDL / VBP monitoring as a recurring source layer.
8. Add quarterly variance analysis that reconciles model forecast, reported actuals, guidance changes and valuation deltas.
