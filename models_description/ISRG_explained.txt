ISRG module explanation
=======================

1. Analysis objective
The ISRG module is a buy-side surgical robotics research cockpit for Intuitive Surgical. It is designed to answer one investment question: is ISRG still a high-quality, durable robotic surgery compounder, or is the equity already pricing near-perfect execution?

2. Why ISRG is not a normal P/E dashboard
ISRG is not modeled as a single revenue/EPS line. The important drivers are installed da Vinci systems, procedure volume, procedures per installed system, instruments and accessories pull-through, service attachment, lease mix, hospital capex ROI, replacement/upgrade cycle, da Vinci 5 adoption, Ion/SP optionality, regulatory/product safety, and competitive pressure. P/E, EV/Sales, EV/EBIT, PEG, and FCF yield are sanity checks only.

3. Core business model
The module treats ISRG as a surgical robotics platform:
Installed Base -> Procedures -> Instruments & Accessories Revenue -> Surgeon/Hospital Familiarity -> Switching Cost -> More Placements and Upgrades.

4. Installed base / procedure / instruments flywheel
The flywheel and procedure dashboards show:
- da Vinci installed-base growth
- worldwide procedure growth
- U.S. vs OUS growth spread when disclosed
- procedures per installed system / utilization
- instruments and accessories revenue per procedure
- recurring revenue mix from I&A plus services
- system ASP proxy and lease mix
- da Vinci 5 placement share and replacement-cycle proxy

5. da Vinci 5
da Vinci 5 matters because it can support an upgrade cycle, potential ASP uplift, workflow efficiency, force feedback, vision, ergonomics, data/insights, and digital surgery optionality. The product narrative remains research-only unless it is mapped to explicit assumptions such as daVinci5ReplacementCycleUplift, systemAspGrowth, utilizationGrowth, operatingMargin, or margin risk.

6. Ion and SP
Ion is handled as a separate early-stage platform, not blended into da Vinci. The cockpit tracks Ion installed base, placements, procedure growth, and probability-weighted optionality. SP is treated as strategic optionality unless disclosed placements/procedures prove scale. Ion/SP value receives an explicit de-duplication haircut so it is not counted both inside the core DCF and as separate TAM value.

7. Hospital capex and customer ROI
The hospital ROI panel tracks operating lease mix, usage-based lease mix, ASP proxy, procedure throughput, surgeon workflow, and upgrade economics. Leasing can lower customer adoption friction while shifting the timing and quality of recognized system revenue.

8. Regulatory and product safety
FDA clearances, CE marks, expanded indications, recalls, MAUDE references, and safety communications are tracked separately. FDA/MAUDE/recall items are research-only until they create validated assumptions for procedure growth, installed-base adoption, ASP, legal cost, or margin.

9. Valuation methods
The valuation triangulation has four layers:
- Procedure-based DCF: Installed Base x Procedures/System x Revenue/Procedure + System Placements x ASP + Service Revenue/System x Installed Base.
- Segment valuation: Instruments & Accessories, Systems, Services, plus haircut Ion/SP optionality.
- Multiple sanity checks: forward P/E, EV/Sales, EV/EBIT, FCF yield, PEG, ROIC-WACC spread, historical percentile if available.
- Bull/Base/Bear scenarios: procedure CAGR, installed-base CAGR, utilization, revenue per procedure, da Vinci 5 upgrade cycle, margin, competition, tariffs, and optionality.

10. Data allowed into valuation
Allowed valuation inputs:
- official earnings release actuals
- SEC filing actuals after frame validation
- official management guidance mapped through assumptions
- analyst forecast assumptions in assumptions.ts / scenarioAssumptions.ts
- derived metrics from official actuals
Market data is used for price, reverse DCF, and sanity checks only.

11. Research-only data
Research-only by default:
- transcripts and Q&A
- management tone and analyst concern tracking
- product narrative
- FDA/MAUDE/recall watch items
- competitor tracker
- GLP-1 and bariatric commentary
- qualitative moat and risk evidence
These can inform the assumption debate, but they cannot directly enter valuation.

12. Major risks
- Procedure growth fades from mid-teens to low-teens.
- da Vinci 5 is mostly replacement rather than TAM expansion.
- Ion fails to become a meaningful second growth curve.
- Hospital capex pressure slows system placements.
- Usage-based leases reduce upfront system revenue recognition.
- Medtronic Hugo, J&J Ottava, CMR Versius, Stryker/Mako adjacency, and China local systems pressure ASP/margins.
- China/Europe tender pressure reduces OUS economics.
- GLP-1 reduces bariatric procedures.
- Tariffs, manufacturing, supply-chain, recalls, litigation, SBC, dilution, or weak buybacks pressure per-share economics.

13. Updating data
Run:
npm run isrg:fetch-official
npm run isrg:fetch-sec
npm run isrg:fetch-transcripts
npm run isrg:build-official-dataset
npm run isrg:build-transcript-intelligence
npm run isrg:build-metrics
npm run isrg:build-qa-pairs

Official artifacts land in data/local/isrg/official, SEC artifacts in data/local/isrg/sec, transcripts in data/local/isrg/transcripts, parsed outputs in data/local/isrg/extracted, market data in data/local/isrg/market, and validation artifacts in data/local/isrg/validation. If a source is blocked or partially parsed, metadata records blocked/parsedSuccessfully rather than pretending success.

14. Validation
Run:
npm run validate:isrg

Validation checks revenue segment reconciliation, ASP proxy, revenue per procedure, procedures per system, installed-base/placement consistency, da Vinci 5 placement subset logic, operating lease subset logic, usage-based lease subset logic, Ion separation, transcript/research-only isolation, scenario assumption isolation, segment double-counting, optionality de-duplication, and bull/base/bear valuation output.

15. Frontend sections
- Cockpit: executive summary, fair value range, reverse DCF, thesis, and risk.
- Flywheel: installed base, procedures, I&A revenue, utilization, switching cost, placements/upgrades.
- Procedure Engine: procedure growth, utilization, guidance, OUS/U.S. spread, growth bridge.
- Installed Base: da Vinci and Ion installed base, placements, lease mix, usage-based leases, replacement proxy.
- Revenue Quality: systems, I&A, services, recurring mix, revenue per procedure, service/system, ASP proxy.
- DV5 / Ion / SP: da Vinci 5 adoption, product-cycle assumptions, Ion S-curve, SP optionality.
- Hospital ROI: customer capex friction, lease adoption, procedure throughput, upgrade economics.
- FDA / Safety: regulatory milestones, recalls/MAUDE watchlist, product safety boundary.
- Valuation Lab: procedure DCF, segment valuation, multiples, scenarios, sensitivities, reverse DCF.
- Competition & Risk: competitor tracker, heatmap, red-team table, kill criteria.
- Transcript Lab: Q&A explorer, topic tracker, analyst concerns, guidance changes.
- Data Boundary: source status, valuation eligibility, research-only rules.
